Specific

Clearly define the goal.

  • Who
  • What
  • Where
  • When
  • Why

Measurable

Criteria to track process and know when the goal has been achieved.

Achievable

Ensure the goal is realistic with your current resources and effort.

Relevant

Confirm the goal aligns with your broader objectives and serves a meaningful purpose.

Time Bound

Set a deadline.
